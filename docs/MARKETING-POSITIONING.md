## Primary Positioning: Career Growth Tool for Developers

### The Insight

Developers journal about their work but struggle to:

- Remember achievements when updating resumes
- Articulate impact during performance reviews
- Prepare compelling interview stories
- Advocate for themselves during promotion cycles

**Papyrus solves this:** Your daily journals become a searchable, AI-powered career database.

## Mental Health Angle (Reframed for Career Growth)

### Original Idea: Emotion tracking, burnout detection

### Reframe: Career Health Signals

**Problem**: You feel drained but don't know if it's normal stress or a signal to change.

**Solution**: Papyrus tracks energy patterns and correlates them with work activities.

**Landing Page Headline:**

> "Is It You, or Is It the Job?"
>
> Track your energy levels. Know when it's time for a change.

**Message:**

- Low energy for weeks → Signal to talk to manager about switching teams
- Stress spikes during certain projects → Signal about what kind of work drains you
- High energy when coding, low during meetings → Signal about role fit

**Positioning**: Not "mental health app", but **"career alignment tool"**

**Example:**

```
Papyrus Insight:

Your energy patterns show:
- High engagement: Backend architecture work, mentoring
- Low engagement: Frontend tasks, long planning meetings

Signal: You might be happier in a backend-focused or tech lead role.
Recommendation: Discuss role evolution with your manager.
```

### Pricing Psychology

**Why These Prices Work:**

| Feature        | Cost to Us | Price | Margin | But Priced On...                 |
| -------------- | ---------- | ----- | ------ | -------------------------------- |
| Promotion Doc  | ~$0.10     | $29   | 290x   | Value of promotion (salary bump) |
| Resume         | ~$0.05     | $19   | 380x   | Value of landing job             |
| Interview Prep | ~$0.10     | $19   | 190x   | Value of interview confidence    |
| Standup (3mo)  | ~$0.60     | $9    | 15x    | Daily convenience value          |

**Key Insight**: Don't price on token cost. Price on outcome value.

- Getting promoted = $10K+ salary bump → $29 is nothing
- Landing job = Huge life change → $19 is trivial
- Daily time savings = 5 min/day × 90 days = 7.5 hours saved → $9 is cheap

---

### Revenue Projection (Rough Estimates)

**Assumptions:**

- 1,000 active free users
- 5% purchase career features per year
- Average career event purchase: $22 (mix of promo/resume/interview)

**Calculation:**

- 1,000 users × 5% conversion = 50 purchases/year
- 50 × $22 = **$1,100/year from career features**

- 20% become daily users → 200 users
- 200 × 50% hit free limit → 100 users
- 100 × $9 (quarterly standup) × 4 quarters = **$3,600/year from standup**

**Total: ~$4,700/year ARR at 1,000 users**

- Scales linearly with users
- Low churn (one-time purchases)
- Upsell path: Try standup → Buy career features when needed

## Retention Strategy (Why Daily Habit Matters)

### The Monetization Flywheel

```
Daily Journal → AI Standup Notes → Habit Formed → User Stays Active
     ↓
More Journals = Better Career Data
     ↓
Career Event (interview, promotion) → User Needs AI Features → Upgrade to Pro
     ↓
Success (got job/promotion) → User Attributes to Papyrus → Shares Story
     ↓
New Users → Cycle Repeats
```

### Why Standup Notes Are Critical

1. **Daily touchpoint** - Users interact with Papyrus every morning
2. **Immediate value** - Saves time right away (no delayed gratification)
3. **Habit formation** - 30 days of daily use = sticky habit
4. **Data accumulation** - More journals = better career features later
5. **Conversion timing** - When users need resume help, they're already engaged

**Key Insight**: Free daily standup notes are NOT a giveaway. They're the **growth engine** that ensures users have rich journal history when they need to upgrade for career features.
